## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- Completed Phase 0 & Phase 1 product investigation covering Intercom, Zendesk, and Chatwoot.
- Generated REA evidence register and 12 comprehensive research and architectural specification documents in `docs/research/`.
- Established repository structure, licensing guidelines (clean-room implementation), and enterprise security requirements.
- Defined relational database schema and multi-tenant isolation strategy with Prisma ORM.
- Outlined MVP implementation roadmap across 8 controlled milestones.
